Mail layout

Every outreach mail is put in the frame below, with its footer and unsubscribe block, in both the HTML and the plain text version.

Used in the footer as {{company_name}} and {{company_address}}. Without a company name the name of the sending account is used; an empty address leaves its line out. Mass mail should show a postal address.
The frame says where the parts go: {{message}} is the mail, {{footer}} the footer, {{unsubscribe}} the unsubscribe block (left out when a mail has no unsubscribe link). Leave the frame empty to stack the three one below the other. Inside the three: {{sender_name}}, {{sender_email}}, {{company_name}}, {{company_address}}, {{unsubscribe_url}}, and the contact placeholders of the campaign mails such as {{name}} and {{company}}.
